HUD Part 58 Environmental Review — New Engagement Intake

About This Form

This form starts a new HUD Part 58 Environmental Review engagement with AZ-RI Consultants. It collects your contact information, the property location, HUD funding details, and the project scope we need to determine the appropriate level of review.

When you submit, a branded PDF summary downloads to you and is sent to AZ-RI. If your engagement is limited to a Phase I or Limited ESA only (no HUD review), please use the ESA Intake Form instead.

5. Level of Review
AZ-RI will determine the appropriate level of review based on the information you provide. HUD environmental reviews are conducted at these levels:
Exempt — activities with no potential environmental impact
CEST — minimal impact requiring basic compliance reviews
EA — projects requiring detailed environmental analysis
EIS — major projects with significant environmental impacts
